<h3>Panel 1:</h3>
A view of Sher with his guitar hanging off his back as he speaks into a microphone. Daniel is watching him from the left, still holding his bass ready to play as Sher speaks to the audience.

<b>Sher:</b> "Before continuing I want to say something, friends. When I visited Earth I was scared of criticizing it. After all they were nice enough to welcome me and criticizing under that light is pretty rude"

"However if I learned one thing from Punk Rock and Metal, it is that you also shouldn't be afraid to say what's wrong, because if nobody hears it, nothing's gonna be done about it."

"I love Earth, I genuinely do. But as someone who comes from a planet that's not developed (yet), where I wish we had all the things Earth already has, it pains me to see how Earth is in a pretty rough shape. And I believe... We believe that you humans can change that! You can...! If you work together. This next song is from our third album named Coordinates and it's called Natural Breath. Let's do this."
