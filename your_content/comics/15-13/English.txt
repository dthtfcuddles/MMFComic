Panel 1 shows Rowi adjusting a microphone next to him. He says "Hello, friends! My name i Rowi. I'm Sher's boyfriend and MMF's drummer. There's something I want to tell you, a story, if I may".

Panel 2 shows an avali grinning. "Almost 20 years ago, I lost a brother. His name was Kirui. His death happened quickly and with no warning. It was extremely painful for those of us left behind, but it was him who inspired us to do whatever made us happy with our lives. I wouldn't be here living as a musician if it wasn't thanks to him. Even in death, he is an inspiration, a model to follow. And I damn well dedicate every show to him and his memory. I am convinced that if he was here, alive with us, he would be damn proud. I know most of us here have lost someone we loved. A sibling, a packmate..."

Panel 3 Rowi starts playing while talking "...This song is for Kirui and every bunny who isn't here with us anymore. This is one of our B-Sides, and one of the first songs we made together as a band. This is called 'Love and Pain', let's do this".

Panel 4 Rowi is the one singing. "We cannot decide what fate has in store, we just need to live with the cards we got. Life, a privilege, living it for them: The ones who can not, the ones who left us".

Panel 5 shows Sher saying "Wo-oh-oah".

Panel 6 Rowi continues "Love and pain are tied, we must live with both, being here with you: Best hand I could draw". The spirit of Kirui is behind Rowi, bopping his head along.
